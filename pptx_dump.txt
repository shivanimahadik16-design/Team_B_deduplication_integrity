WHAT WE BUILT & WHY IT MATTERS
Team B is the deterministic Data-Structures engine of the Smart File Backup System: it chunks files, hashes and de-duplicates them, indexes chunks with swappable HashMap/AVL/Red-Black structures, and proves integrity with Merkle trees — cutting stored bytes while keeping every backup verifiable.
